How it runs and the guarantee

Per Parker, you already have 12 mailboxes — we start sending with those on day one. In parallel we add 24 more, which need around 3 weeks to warm up. After that, we can guarantee the numbers above. Zaymo owns every mailbox we set up, and you can cancel them monthly any time. Minimum 10 qualified meetings every month. If we miss in any month, that month's retainer is refunded in full.

What we run for you

Sourcing, qualification, copy, sending, forwarding replies to Parker, and the weekly call. All built inside your Instantly and Clay accounts. You own every part of it and can keep running it on your own whether you renew with us or not.

What we need from you

A 30-minute weekly call with Parker, a shared Slack channel, and budget for 24 more Instantly mailboxes (~$100 to $150 / month, paid by Zaymo, owned by Zaymo, cancellable any time). The call is the one thing we can't do without — it's how we figure out what a high-fit Zaymo buyer actually looks like, and why month 2 always beats month 1.

Risk assessment

If we hit 10+ in a month

Net +$3,100 to +$3,900 captured that month on new MRR vs. retainer. Engagement continues. Infrastructure stays with Zaymo.

If we miss 10 in a month

That month's retainer is refunded in full. You keep every account, mailbox, sequence, and prospect list we built. Inbox subscription is monthly-cancellable.

Worst case: if you walk after a missed month, you're only out about $200 to $300 of inbox spend — and that isn't wasted either. The mailboxes still work, they're yours, and you can cancel them monthly any time.

You research what already works in the category of software in scope (vertical SaaS / ops / compliance), so the operator borrows the proven evaluation lens instead of guessing.

Deliver (sourced):
1. **VC/operator selection criteria** for a good niche — underserved, fragmented-but-not-impossible, enough depth for the MRR/ARR target, moderate tech-adopters, money flowing through the workflow, system-of-record potential. Cite sources.
2. **Proven winners by industry** — table (company | industry | size | the one lesson).
3. **"X-proven → local-gap" map** — models proven in market X (often US) without a funded champion in the operator's geography.
4. **The macro/regulatory wave** creating mandatory, recurring demand — and its fragility (policy/subsidy dependence).
5. **1–2 reference archetypes** — a concrete company embodying the winning pattern + which pattern to copy (and the bootstrap-vs-funded tension it implies).
6. **Numbered source list.**

Return as your final message. Concise, concrete, no vanity-TAM.
